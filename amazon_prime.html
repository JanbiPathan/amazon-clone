Amazon Prime

Amazon Prime is a subscription service that gives members access to exclusive deals, free fast delivery, video streaming, music streaming, and more. Launched in 2005, Amazon Prime has become a central part of Amazon's strategy to retain loyal customers.

Prime Benefits

  • Fast and Free Shipping
  • Amazon Prime Video: Exclusive TV shows, movies, and documentaries
  • Prime Music: Ad-free music streaming
  • Prime Reading: Access to a large selection of books
  • Exclusive Deals and Discounts

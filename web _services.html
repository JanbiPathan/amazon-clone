Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world’s most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud platform. Launched in 2006, AWS offers over 200 fully featured services from data centers globally. Millions of customers—including the fastest-growing startups, largest enterprises, and leading government agencies—use AWS to lower costs, become more agile, and innovate faster.

Why AWS?

  • Scalability: Instantly scale your infrastructure to meet your needs.
  • Security: Comprehensive security capabilities to meet the most stringent requirements.
  • Cost-Effective: Pay only for what you use, with no upfront costs.
  • Global Reach: Operate seamlessly around the world.

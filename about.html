About Amazon

Amazon, founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994, started as an online bookstore and quickly evolved into one of the largest e-commerce platforms globally. With innovations in technology, customer service, and logistics, Amazon has redefined how people shop online.

History

Initially, Amazon's goal was to be "the world's most customer-centric company," offering customers everything from books to electronics. Over the years, the company has expanded into multiple sectors, including cloud computing (AWS), entertainment (Amazon Prime Video), and more.

The Future

Amazon continues to push boundaries with developments in drone delivery, artificial intelligence, and more. The future of the company is focused on sustainability, innovation, and providing the best shopping experience for customers worldwide.

Key Milestones

  • 1994: Amazon founded by Jeff Bezos
  • 1997: Amazon goes public
  • 2005: Launch of Amazon Prime
  • 2013: Acquisition of The Washington Post
  • 2020: Amazon becomes a trillion-dollar company
